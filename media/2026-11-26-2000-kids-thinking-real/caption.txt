Who's doing your child's thinking?

Kids ask AI about their homework, their friends, even their future. Half of kids who use AI already ask it about what's next for them.

What the research shows:
• In an MIT study, people who wrote with ChatGPT struggled to quote their own essay minutes later.
• In a study of 666 people, the youngest leaned on AI most and scored lowest on critical thinking.
• 1,506 people wrote with an AI that leaned one way. It shifted what they wrote and what they believed, and most never noticed.
• With AI ideas, each story got better, but all the stories became more alike.

My prediction: the rare kid who learns to think first will be the one who leads.

The rule: think first, then ask. And before your kid asks AI, ask them: what do YOU think?
