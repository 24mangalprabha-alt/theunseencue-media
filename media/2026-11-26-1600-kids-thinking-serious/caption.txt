Who is really doing your child's thinking?

Kids now ask AI about their homework, their friends, even their future. Half of kids who use AI already ask it what's next for them.

What the research shows:
• MIT: people who wrote with ChatGPT struggled to quote their own essay right after.
• 666 people: the youngest leaned on AI most and scored lowest on critical thinking.
• 1,506 people wrote with an AI that leaned one way. It shifted what they wrote and what they believed. Most never noticed.
• With AI ideas, every story got better, but all the stories became more alike.

Now zoom out. Stanford counted 94 leading AI models last year from just two countries. A few machines, billions of minds.

So who is shaping our future? Or will we still think for ourselves?
